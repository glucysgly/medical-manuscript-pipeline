# Writing frameworks

Frameworks are scaffolds, not paragraph generators. Choose the smallest structure that makes the real argument visible.

## Introduction: five-part funnel

1. Define the clinical or biological problem.
2. Explain why the existing evidence is insufficient.
3. Introduce the specific factor, exposure, method, or dataset.
4. State the exact knowledge gap.
5. State what this study did to address it.

Merge parts when the field is simple. Do not repeat the same importance claim in several forms.

## Methods: evidence-layer order

Move from design and evidence layers to participants/datasets, measurements or assays, outcomes and definitions, analysis, quality controls, sensitivity analyses, ethics, and availability. Include details that permit interpretation or reproduction; keep internal project administration elsewhere.

## Results: evidence staircase

Move from study flow and sample description to the primary finding, independent or complementary evidence, sensitivity/robustness evidence, negative or boundary findings, and a short factual summary. Do not mix interpretation into every result paragraph.

## Discussion: adaptable James five-step method

1. State the main findings and their scale without replaying all Results.
2. Compare them with the most relevant prior evidence, including meaningful agreement or disagreement.
3. Explain the study's contribution and propose a plausible, testable interpretation where justified.
4. Address unexpected, negative, or conflicting findings and reasonable alternatives.
5. Concentrate limitations, clinical/scientific meaning, and the most decisive next step.

The five steps describe functions, not a mandatory five-paragraph layout. Combine or expand steps according to the study. An important surprise may appear earlier if that is the natural place to interpret it. Keep hypotheses labelled as hypotheses.

## Conclusion: four functions

State the main result, its clinical or biological boundary, the evidence level, and the next validation that would change confidence or practice. Usually this needs only a few sentences.

## Reviewer response

Use: reviewer point, action taken, evidence checked or added, manuscript location, and remaining boundary. Respond directly; do not convert every response into a defensive paragraph in the paper.
