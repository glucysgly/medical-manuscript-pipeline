# Systematic review branch

Use a protocol-led path: question and eligibility, databases and reproducible searches, deduplication, dual screening or recorded adjudication, full-text decisions, extraction, risk of bias, synthesis plan, heterogeneity, certainty where applicable, PRISMA accounting, and update date.

Keep search results, screening decisions, evidence extraction, and manuscript prose separate. Every included-study count must reconcile across the flow diagram, tables, text, and excluded-full-text record. Do not write the narrative conclusion before the included evidence and bias assessment are frozen.

For meta-analysis, predefine effect measure, unit, model, heterogeneity, sensitivity/subgroup rules, and publication-bias limits. Do not convert observational association into intervention efficacy or causal certainty.
