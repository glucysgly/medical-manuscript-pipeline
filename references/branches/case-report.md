# Case report branch

Build the clinical timeline first. Freeze presentation, relevant history, examination, investigations, differential diagnosis, intervention, outcome, follow-up, and patient perspective when available. De-identify all material and obtain the required consent before submission.

The contribution should arise from diagnostic reasoning, an unusual presentation, management insight, adverse event, or a clearly bounded lesson. Do not claim frequency, efficacy, or causality from one case. Use a CARE-style completeness check and keep the narrative clinically readable rather than turning it into a chart dump.
