# Clinical trial branch

Freeze registration, protocol version, prespecified outcomes, allocation, masking, sample-size assumptions, analysis population, deviations, harms, participant flow, recruitment dates, and statistical analysis plan before manuscript interpretation.

Keep prespecified, amended, post hoc, and exploratory analyses visibly distinct. Reconcile registration, protocol, SAP, tables, figures, CONSORT flow, abstract, and manuscript. Report effect estimates and uncertainty, not P values alone. Do not use prose to hide outcome switching, incomplete follow-up, or harms.

Ethics, consent, registration identifier, data monitoring, funding, sponsor role, and data-sharing statements are submission gates, not optional style details.
