# Public-data bioinformatics branch

Freeze accession, release date, sample metadata, biological unit, inclusion/exclusion, preprocessing level, annotation version, batch structure, contrasts, statistical unit, multiple-testing method, and code/environment. Keep public-data findings distinct from local experiments or clinical validation.

Treat dataset selection and sample mapping as scientific decisions. Check donor or subject independence, duplicated cohorts, platform differences, hidden confounding, and whether validation datasets are truly independent. Report effect size, uncertainty, heterogeneity, and direction; do not present correlation, co-expression, enrichment, or predicted networks as demonstrated mechanism.

Use processed data when they answer the question reproducibly; do not download large raw data by default. Keep code-driven figures and a machine-readable result source so manuscript numbers are not manually re-entered.
