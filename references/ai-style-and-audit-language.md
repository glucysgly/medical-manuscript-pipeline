# AI-style and audit-language control

## Timing

Run this pass after scientific freeze. Style edits before evidence, analysis, and logic are stable create rework and can conceal scientific changes.

## Preserve exactly

Preserve sample sizes, numerical results, signs and directions, effect estimates, uncertainty, P values, units, group labels, technical identifiers, gene/protein distinctions, table/figure numbering, citations, and conclusion strength unless an upstream gate is formally reopened.

## Remove or rewrite

- repeated mini-summaries and repeated disclaimers;
- symmetrical paragraph templates used regardless of content;
- excessive headings, numbered rhetoric, and stock transitions;
- inflated novelty, vague importance, and promotional adjectives;
- rare synonyms used only to avoid repetition;
- long noun stacks and complex grammar that the author would not normally use;
- audit or project-management terms such as provenance audit, canonical manifest, reconciliation pipeline, candidate register, gate passed, or source lock when the reader only needs the scientific fact;
- claims assembled from adjacent facts without an evidence link.

Translate internal control into reader-facing science. For example, replace a description of “source reconciliation” with the actual inclusion rule, corrected identifier, assay source, or sensitivity analysis when that fact matters.

## Keep when scientifically necessary

Do not delete genuine uncertainty, negative results, heterogeneity, missing data, protocol deviations, measurement constraints, or material conflicts merely because they sound cautious. State each once, in the section where it changes interpretation.

## Final style questions

1. Does every paragraph have a scientific or clinical job?
2. Would the logic remain clear with fewer connectors?
3. Are common words available for any decorative term?
4. Does one stable author seem to have written every section?
5. Does the prose explain the study rather than document the workflow?
6. Did any style edit change a fact, number, citation, or evidence ceiling?

Do not claim that a manuscript is undetectable as AI-written. The goal is accurate, natural, author-consistent scientific prose.
